## 1. Selection register

| Artifact | Owner | Template variant | Requested? | Selected module IDs | Recommendation and rationale | Final scope state |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[Requested strategy or other artifact]` | `[owner]` | `[library template or explicit Full/Compact]` | `[yes/no]` | `[IDs or All]` | `[Required/Recommended/Optional + why]` | `[selected/not selected/deferred]` |

- Strategy selection -> use the approved project strategy templates for the requested strategy types and module mappings. General marketing strategy uses its Marketing Strategy template; `Full` and `Compact` remain alternatives when explicitly requested.
- Each selected strategy gets its own row and output. Overlap is permitted; selection of one does not select or exclude another.
- `All` expands to the sections and mapped modules of the selected template. A subset includes only named sections/modules. Record document type plus module ID for exact question and readiness mapping.
- Do not add a deliverable or module after approval without versioning this matrix and extending the Brief question set.
