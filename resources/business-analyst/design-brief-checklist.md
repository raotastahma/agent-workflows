# Design Brief Checklist

Use only when design ownership is `agent-created`.

- screens/pages
- primary flows
- brand constraints
- visual references
- liked/disliked products
- preferred tone/style
- platform constraints
- design-system/framework limits
- content constraints
- real copy vs placeholder
- localization needs
- required states: empty/loading/error/success/permissions; cover every state reachable in the scoped flows
- accessibility/compliance/domain UX constraints
- expected deliverable

If any item materially affects design decisions, ask during BA discovery.
