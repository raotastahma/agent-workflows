# Quality Gates

Apply every mandatory gate before delivery. Fix failures; do not average them into a score.

## Gate 1: Evidence

- Every material external claim has a traceable source and as-of date.
- Every slide with a claim based on external research or authoritative online data includes a small, readable `Source:` or `Sources:` line at the bottom; each source label is clickable and opens the exact supporting page.
- Founder-provided facts are not presented as independently verified.
- Estimates expose their inputs and formulas; assumptions and targets are labeled.
- No fabricated traction, customer quote, partnership, credential, competitor claim, market figure, or legal/visa eligibility claim remains.
- Data gaps that change the investment case are resolved or explicitly disclosed.

## Gate 2: Model

- Revenue follows the selected business drivers.
- Inputs, formulas, outputs, scenarios, and sources are separated and readable.
- Forecast formulas copy consistently across periods.
- Downside, base, and upside recalculate from drivers.
- Cash, burn, runway, ask, uses, and milestones reconcile.
- Formula-error scan is clean and all required `Checks` rows pass.
- Every user-facing sheet has been rendered and visually inspected.

## Gate 3: Deck-to-Model Reconciliation

- Every projected or financial slide value maps to an exact workbook range.
- Deck and workbook use the same scenario, currency, units, period, and definition.
- Charts use the same values as their labels and accompanying copy.
- Rounding differences are presentation-only and do not change the conclusion.
- Market sizing, business plan, roadmap milestones, and ask do not contradict one another.

## Gate 4: Content and Narrative

- User-required structure and slide counts are preserved.
- Each slide has one decision-relevant takeaway.
- Every slide title is a one-line, plain-language conclusion; a reader without specialized knowledge understands the slide's subject and point at first glance, and reading only the titles communicates the argument.
- Text leads with the answer or conclusion, follows a clear source-backed sequence, keeps one idea per paragraph or text block, and ends standalone answers or narratives with a grounded conclusion.
- Every sentence adds evidence, explanation, choice, or argument; copy uses concrete detail, simple language, and a measured tone, explains unavoidable terms, and removes filler, repetition, hype, and unsupported claims.
- When material, founder roles are distinct and complementary, and product focus is justified as a practical choice.
- The em dash character (`-`) does not appear anywhere.
- Product, innovation, differentiation, France relevance, and impact claims explain why they matter and how they are evidenced.
- The ask states amount, runway, uses, and measurable milestones.
- Speaker notes add context, caveats, methodology, and sources without exposing internal drafting instructions.

## Gate 5: Visual Quality

- One coherent visual system governs typography, color, spacing, charts, imagery, and page markers.
- Every accent color communicates meaning, grouping, hierarchy, or intended focus; no accent is decorative only.
- Adjacent slides vary composition without breaking the system.
- Product evidence and data dominate over decoration.
- No unintended overlap, clipping, wrapping, unresolved placeholder, blurry crop, broken connector, or inconsistent footer remains.
- No slide title wraps to two lines.
- Body text remains presentation-readable; shorten copy or change layout before shrinking it.
- Every final slide is rendered and inspected individually at full size.

## Gate 6: Delivery

- Final deck and workbook are editable and open successfully.
- Revision requests were applied to the current deck or workbook in place; a separate file was created only when explicitly requested, with the current file left unchanged.
- Slide notes and workbook sources provide an audit trail.
- Scratch plans, temporary renders, builders, and internal ledgers are not delivered unless requested.

Any mandatory gate failure -> do not deliver as final. Fix it or report the exact blocker and affected claim, slide, sheet, or range.
